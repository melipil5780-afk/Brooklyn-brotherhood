Agreements
Brooklyn Brotherhood I-Group
📜 Open Full Agreements Doc

By committing to these agreements, each man is "taking a seat at the table" — building a strong container based on shared MKP values.

```
1Guiding Principles›
1.1 MKP Code of Ethics

Men are expected to uphold Confidentiality, Punctuality, Ethical Relationships, Non-Violence, and Prohibition of Drugs or Alcohol. If a man is under the influence, he will inform the group and accept any decision regarding his continued presence.

1.2 Brotherhood Guiding Principles

Creating a safe and sacred space for men to share, grow, and be seen · Fostering brotherhood, trust, and accountability · Developing leadership skills and deeper connections · Building a diverse and inclusive community that supports each other's healing and growth.

2Attendance & Membership›
2.1 Welcoming Period

Complete an NWTA weekend, agree to these agreements, attend 3 meetings + 1 social within 3 months. A unanimous vote is required. Only 1 brand-new guest per meeting; they must have had a screening call with a standing member first.

2.2 Membership Cap

Capped at 12 members. If the cap is reached, the group is "closed" — no new guests or members until a spot opens.

2.3 Dues

$35/month due to the Treasurer by the 1st. Men on a leave of absence (2+ months) are not expected to pay.

2.4 RSVPs & Punctuality

RSVP by 7 PM Sunday prior to each meeting via Google Calendar. Meeting is closed for men arriving 30+ minutes late. After 2 months of no attendance without a leave of absence, the man's seat is given up.

3Meeting Logistics›
Location, Date & Time

The Loft at Park Slope Performing Arts · 135 Windsor Pl, Brooklyn, NY 11215 · 2nd & 4th Wednesdays · 7–9 PM. We are still responsible for paying for the room even if a meeting is cancelled due to low RSVPs.

Group Socials

First Wednesday of each month, planned by the Social Coordinator. Open to core members, men in their welcoming period, and invited guests.

Cancellations

Fewer than 3 RSVPs = meeting cancelled, announced via WhatsApp.

4Roles & Responsibilities›
Facilitation

Upon accepting the role of facilitator for another man's work, only that man may perform that role. The facilitator may request support. All other men present must hold sacred space.

King for Each Meeting

The King emails the group by end-of-day Saturday. The Secretary prepares the email. At the Business Round, the King requests a volunteer for the next meeting; if no one steps up, the King selects someone.

Administrative Roles

Terms span several months. At the final meeting of each quarter, a new man steps up for any expiring roles.

5Amendments & Minor Edits›
Amendments

Changes that affect group functioning require a majority vote (>50%) in person or electronically (WhatsApp, Google Forms).

Minor Edits

Changes that don't affect functioning may occur without a vote, but must be discussed with the I-Group Representative first. The I-Group Rep sends written notice to the group.

Sending account: thebrooklynbrotherhood@gmail.com
I-Group listserv: brooklynbrotherhood-igroup@mkpconnect.org
```
